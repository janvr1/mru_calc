DVGW G600 / G5952 Leak Rate Matrix Calculator

DVGW G600 Assessment Matrix (Gebrauchsfähigkeit)

Leak Rate < 1.0 l/h: Unrestricted Usability (Gebrauchsfähigkeit gegeben) - System is safe for continuous operation.
Leak Rate 1.0 l/h to < 5.0 l/h: Reduced Usability (Verminderte Gebrauchsfähigkeit) - The system must be professionally repaired or sealed within 4 weeks.[
Leak Rate ≥ 5.0 l/h: No Usability / Danger (Keine Gebrauchsfähigkeit) - The system must be taken out of service immediately.
* Pipe lengths are estimated based on typical standard European copper gas pipes: 3/4" (22x1mm) ≈ 0.314 l/m; 1" (28x1.5mm) ≈ 0.491 l/m.
